The problem

Clinics, patients and records all work alone.

The consult is the only place where a doctor, a patient and a record meet — and it is the most time-starved moment in healthcare.

Doctors drown in intake

History, old reports, medicine lists and notes eat the consult. The clinical decision gets whatever time is left.

Patients wait, then guess

At 2 AM the choices are a symptom checker with a disclaimer or a queue in the morning. Nobody is accountable for the answer.

Records live in silos

Vitals in a wearable app, labs on paper, prescriptions on WhatsApp. The next doctor starts from a blank page.

The gap

Every tool does one part. None closes the loop.

Ask, triage, doctor, prescription, record, reminder — today that is four apps and a phone call. HealthEngage CPM runs it as one thread.

How it works

One thread, five steps, zero hand-offs lost.

1

Patient asks

Any symptom, any hour, in their own words. Add a photo, a lab report or an old prescription.

2

AI prepares

Follow-up questions, red flags, extracted reports and wearable vitals become a structured case summary.

3

Doctor decides

Differentials, guideline-based options and safety checks on screen. The doctor confirms, edits and signs.

4

Record keeps

Signed Rx, consult note and reports land in the patient's vault — shareable with any doctor, revocable any time.

5

Reach follows up

Medicine, refill and test reminders in-app; a voice call from Reach when a reminder is ignored.
